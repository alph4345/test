This folder is the website, ready to upload. Upload everything in it
except this note, including .htaccess and the maps folder.

Double-clicking index.html shows a bare page: the pages link to
/style.css, /drops and so on, and "/" means the top of the website on
a web server but the top of the drive on your computer.

To see the site on your computer first, double-click PREVIEW.cmd in the
pn0va-site folder, or drag this folder onto it. It shows this folder
the way the web host will, Drops map included, with nothing to install.
