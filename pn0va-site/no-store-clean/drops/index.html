Transmission—

Placed at dusk, halfway up a staircase most people take at a jog. If you hear the flock before you see it, you have gone too far.

Take the item, leave something of equal or greater mystery, and sign the logbook. Bring your own pen — the last one walked off.

This one required a boat ride to place. It sits within sight of one of the most photographed views around, tucked where thousands of people walk past every week without a second glance.

Hidden in plain sight, exactly the way I like it.

Somewhere among the trees west of the city, beneath a landmark that has watched over the spot for more than a century. I hid it here because this is where the whole idea started — the first place I ever went looking for something that wasn't on any map.

Recovered 2026.06.28. The logbook stays behind for the next visitor.
